# Data Processing Agreement

This DPA forms part of the Terms of Service.

## Scope
One Big Team processes personal data solely on documented customer instructions.

## Security
Appropriate technical and organisational measures are maintained.

## Confidentiality
Personnel authorised to process data are subject to confidentiality obligations.

## Subprocessors
Approved subprocessors may be used. A current list is maintained separately.

## Assistance
We will assist customers with applicable data subject requests and security obligations where reasonably required.

## Deletion
Upon termination, customer data will be deleted or returned in accordance with contractual and legal requirements.
