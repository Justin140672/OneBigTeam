# Privacy Policy

## Introduction
One Big Team ("we", "our", "us") provides cloud-based HR, recruitment and workforce management software for business customers.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data in accordance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Roles
For customer employee and applicant data, your organisation is the Data Controller and One Big Team acts as the Data Processor.

For account, billing and website information, One Big Team acts as the Data Controller.

## Information We Collect
- Account information
- Company information
- Employee records
- Recruitment data
- Uploaded documents
- Audit logs
- Billing information
- Technical and diagnostic information

## How We Use Information
- Deliver the Service
- Authenticate users
- Provide customer support
- Maintain security
- Meet legal obligations
- Improve the Service

## Legal Bases
Performance of a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations and consent where required.

## Data Sharing
We use trusted subprocessors to provide infrastructure and operational services. A current list is available on the Subprocessors page.

## International Transfers
Where transfers occur outside the UK, appropriate safeguards are implemented.

## Security
We apply technical and organisational measures including encryption, authentication, least-privilege access and audit logging.

## Retention
Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service or meet legal obligations.

## Your Rights
Individuals may exercise applicable rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability via their employer or directly where appropriate.
