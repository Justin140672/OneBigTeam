# Privacy Policy

## Who we are

One Big Team is a trading name of Crazy Cat Software Limited, registered in England and Wales under company number 15029972. Our registered office is 2nd Floor, Market House, Market Square, Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire HP20 1TN.

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit our website, contact us, create or administer an account, or use One Big Team. Contact us at [privacy@onebigteam.co.uk](mailto:privacy@onebigteam.co.uk).

## Our role

For employee, worker, applicant and other HR data entered by a customer, the customer is normally the controller and One Big Team acts as its processor. Questions or rights requests about that information should normally be directed to the relevant employer or organisation. Our [Data Processing Agreement](/data-processing-agreement) governs that processing.

Crazy Cat Software Limited is the controller for information used to operate our own business, including website enquiries, trial and account administration, billing, security, supplier management and direct communications.

## Information we handle as controller

We may collect:

- Your name, work email, organisation, job role and contact details;
- Account, authentication and service-administration information;
- Enquiries, support requests and correspondence;
- Subscription, invoice, payment-status and transaction information. Payment-card details are collected directly by Stripe and are not stored by One Big Team;
- Service usage, security, audit, device and diagnostic information, including IP address and browser information; and
- Cookie and browser-storage information described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

We normally obtain this information from you, your organisation, your use of the service, and service providers involved in authentication, billing, hosting, security and communications.

## Why we use it and our lawful bases

We use personal data:

- To create and administer trials, accounts and subscriptions, provide requested services and communicate about them — necessary to perform a contract or take steps at your request before entering one;
- To bill customers, maintain business and tax records, and comply with regulatory duties — necessary to comply with legal obligations;
- To secure, monitor, troubleshoot and improve the service; prevent misuse and fraud; provide support; and manage our business relationships — necessary for our legitimate interests in operating a secure and effective business, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights;
- To respond to enquiries — necessary to take requested steps, perform a contract, or for our legitimate interests in communicating with prospective and existing customers; and
- For optional marketing or non-essential storage technologies where used — with consent where the law requires it. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

We do not use personal data for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

## Customer-controlled HR information

Customers may use the service to process employee records, contact details, employment and compensation information, leave and sickness records, recruitment information, documents, emergency contacts, audit history and equal-opportunities monitoring information. This can include special-category data. The relevant customer determines why that information is processed, who may access it and the applicable lawful basis.

## Sharing information

We disclose personal data only where necessary to:

- Provide the service using the providers listed on our [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page;
- Process subscription payments and prevent fraud;
- Obtain professional advice, insurance or business support under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
- Comply with law, court orders or lawful requests; or
- Protect the rights, safety and security of One Big Team, our customers or others.

We do not sell personal data.

## International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where a restricted transfer occurs, we use a lawful transfer mechanism such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with a transfer risk assessment and additional safeguards where required.

## Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including legal, accounting, security and dispute-resolution requirements.

- Customer service data remains available during the subscription and for a 30-day recovery period after cancellation, then active copies are deleted;
- Residual customer data may remain in encrypted backups for up to a further 30 days;
- Generated customer exports expire after seven days;
- Website enquiries and support correspondence are periodically reviewed and deleted when no longer needed;
- Account, transaction and billing records may be retained for the period required by tax, accounting and legal rules; and
- Security and audit records are retained for a proportionate period based on their purpose and risk.

The customer controls retention of HR records while its account remains active and is responsible for applying appropriate retention periods to those records.

## Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards including authentication, role-based access, organisation isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, private document storage, audit logging, monitoring and backups. More information is available on our [Security](/security) page.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your personal data; object to processing; receive portable data; and withdraw consent. These rights are not absolute and exemptions may apply.

For information controlled by your employer or another One Big Team customer, contact that organisation first. For information controlled by us, email [privacy@onebigteam.co.uk](mailto:privacy@onebigteam.co.uk). We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/). We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first.

## Whether you must provide information

Information marked as required during registration, billing or support is needed to provide the relevant service or respond to your request. If it is not provided, we may be unable to create an account, supply the service or answer the enquiry. Optional fields are identified where applicable.

## Changes

We may update this policy when our services, suppliers or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised version and its updated date, and provide additional notice where a change materially affects how we use personal data.
