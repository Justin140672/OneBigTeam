# Sickness Management

> This is an early summary document. `15-sickness-management.md` is the authoritative Sickness
> capability document; where this document conflicts with it, `15-sickness-management.md` takes

## Purpose
Track sickness absences, fit notes, and return-to-work workflows.

## Business Rules
- Partial-day sickness supported
- Fit note threshold configurable
- Attendance alerts informational only

## Events
- sickness_recorded
- fit_note_uploaded
- return_to_work_completed
